# Protocol draft — negative geotaxis

Status: draft. Do not run until a host lab signs the local version.

1. Age-match adult flies (e.g. 3–5 days), mixed or sex-sorted, n ≥ 10 per vial, ≥ 3 vials per dose.
2. Vehicle vs compound at 3 concentrations spanning the in-silico EC50.
3. Exposure route must be stated (food, vapour, injection). Food mixing is the default teaching route.
4. Tap vial, record number above a 8 cm mark at 10 s. Repeat 3 trials, 1 min rest.
5. Blind the scorer if possible.
6. Export means to a FlyLab notebook under `live_lab`.
7. Fit at most one simulator parameter (`alpha` or EC50). Report the residual.
