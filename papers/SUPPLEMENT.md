## S1. Mechanism rules: the gain patch and its rationale

`flylab/pharm/mechanisms.py` is the single source of truth mapping engagement rows onto circuit gains; no assay carries its own copy, which is what makes Table T2 a truthful description of the software. Gains are dimensionless multipliers on the synaptic weight of a transmitter class (or, for `g_nav`, on node excitability); 1.0 is vehicle, and every gain is floored at 0.05 because a network with a gain of exactly zero is not informative. The 15 rules are listed in full in T2. The four shipped since v0.4 may not be changed silently:

* **Insect nAChR, activating:** `g_ach = max(0.05, 1 + 0.4θ − 1.6θ²)`. Rises at low engagement and falls at high engagement, encoding excitation followed by desensitisation and depolarisation block. At θ = 0.99 it is at the floor, which is why a saturating dose of a nicotinic agonist silences cholinergic transmission in the model rather than merely reducing it — and, as §3.4 of the manuscript shows, why the suppression conclusion is a property of *this* rule.
* **Insect nAChR, blocking:** `g_ach = max(0.05, 1 − θ)`.
* **Insect RDL, blocking:** `g_gaba = max(0.05, 1 − θ)`. Blocking the GABA-gated chloride channel removes inhibition.
* **Insect RDL, activating:** `g_gaba = max(0.05, 1 + 0.4θ)`.

Four families added in v0.5 share the shape and state a rationale each: GluCl activation uses a larger coefficient (`1 + 0.8θ`) than RDL because avermectin opening is effectively irreversible [37]; AChE inhibition raises an ambient cholinergic tone (`ach_tone = 1 + 2.0θ`) multiplying `g_ach` together with the agonist curve, reproducing the organophosphate excitation-then-block picture; Nav modulation scales node excitability globally (`g_nav = 1 + 1.5θ`), since pyrethroids and DDT act on every cell regardless of transmitter [22], [66]; octopamine agonism uses the smallest coefficient (`1 + 0.5θ`) because it is modulatory.

**The level-C generic rule.** The ablation ladder's topology-only level is given one number per compound instead of a mechanism-specific gain vector. Until v0.6.1 that number was `g_all = max(0.05, 1 - theta_max)`, applied to every transmitter alike — a rule that can only depress, so every one of its 21 × 4 entries was at most zero and the level could not express disinhibition at all. It is now `g_all = max(0.05, 1 - theta_max) if the compound's dominant mechanism lowers net synaptic drive, else 1 + theta_max`: the same generic magnitude, with one bit of sign read off the mechanism table. The historical rule: max(0.05, 1 - theta_max) on every transmitter. Structurally incapable of a positive effect, so it cannot order a library containing RDL/GluCl blockers. Reported as a FLOOR (what a depression-only connectome model achieves), never as a competitor. Both are reported, the old one as `C_topology_only_floor` in T13 and T13b.

**These coefficients and shapes are modelling choices, not measurements.** That is why the manuscript prespecifies a family of 25 alternatives (§2.4) and re-derives every conclusion under each, and why the variance analysis nominates this transformation as the assumption worth measuring first. The **transmitter sign table** the rate engine iterates is a second set of asserted coefficients and is published as T21: 5 of the seven magnitudes (glutamate -0.4, histamine -0.5, dopamine +0.2, serotonin +0.2, octopamine +0.2) carry no measurement, are members of no specification family, and are not bounded by the label-permuting null, which permutes assignments and leaves the table itself untouched.

1. *The shuffle count could not reject.* The topology predicates were evaluated at 6 shuffles per specification, where the smallest attainable empirical permutation probability is 1/7 = 0.143. `p ≤ 0.05` was arithmetically unattainable, so the predicate fell back on a Gaussian `|z| < 2` and C3's 25/25 was a non-rejection under a test that could not reject. The run behind this document uses 100 shuffles per specification (resolution 0.010), which supports α = 0.050 with room to spare.

Every specification therefore fed the **default** gains to its nulls and produced identical effects, so a uniform retention rate across the family was guaranteed by construction. The dependence engine now accepts the gain patch explicitly (`gains_by_compound`), and the per-specification topology verdicts in T14 are computed under the specification they are labelled with.

Both topology conclusions survive the correction (25/25 and 25/25 after Benjamini-Hochberg across the 100 structural tests of the run; 25 and 25 uncorrected), but a retention rate for a conclusion shaped as a *failure to reject* is only as strong as the equivalence behind it. Of C3's 25, **17 reach equivalence within the prespecified margin and 8 are merely indeterminate**; the corresponding split for C4 is 0 / 0. Every specification's topology cell is run on the same shuffle stream, so the structural tests are positively dependent rather than independent; Benjamini-Hochberg controls the FDR under positive dependence, but the fractions describe this shuffle stream and are not 25 independent replications.

## S2. Null-model definitions, and the conventions around them

`flylab/analysis/nullmodels.py` and `flylab/analysis/dependence.py` degrade the same committed cut, in increasing order of destruction:

* **`sign_permute_weight_matched`** — a constrained shuffle of consensus transmitter labels: random pairs of differently-labelled nodes are swapped, and a swap is kept only if every tracked transmitter's share of *total outgoing synaptic weight* stays within 0.002 of the real graph's. The node count of each label is preserved exactly, the weighted share to within the tolerance. This is the ladder's rank-3 rung.
* **`sign_permute`** — the plain permutation of the node label array. It is a **joint target-set-and-sign null**, not a rung: see below.
* **`weight_permute`** — synapse counts are permuted across edges. Topology and transmitters are kept; the pairing between wiring and strength is destroyed.
* **`rewire_degree_preserving`** — double-edge swaps, roughly 10 × |E| accepted swaps. Every node keeps its in-degree, out-degree and transmitter; the wiring pattern is destroyed.
* **`erdos_renyi`** — the same node and edge counts, endpoints drawn uniformly, weights drawn from the empirical weight list. Only the size of the graph and its census survive.

**Why `sign_permute` is not a transmitter-identity null.** The v0.6 supplement stated that permuting labels preserves the transmitter histogram "and therefore the global excitation/inhibition balance, preserved *exactly*". That is wrong, and the error was load-bearing: out-strength is heterogeneous across nodes, so the *weighted* balance moves. Measured (T23), the cholinergic share of total outgoing synaptic weight on the `named` cut is 0.619 on the real graph against 0.544 ± 0.027 across 1000 permutations, range 0.461–0.616, putting the real graph at the 100th percentile of its own null. Because a gain patch acts *through* transmitter identity, the mode moves the drug's target set and the sign matrix at once, and the extra variance makes it conservative. The weight-matched null holds every tracked share to within 0.002. Substituting it does not change imidacloprid's permutation probability but it shrinks the equivalence gap from 2.652 rate-model units to 0.236 rate-model units against a margin of 0.332 rate-model units.

Every mode preserves the node set, the node order and the seed block, and never creates self-loops or duplicate edges. The effect is always `treated − vehicle` with **both arms on the same graph**. The reported probability is the empirical two-sided permutation *p*, `(k+1)/(n+1)`, with resolution `1/(n+1)`; on a landscape the same `n` shuffled graphs are reused by every cell (a paired design), and shuffle *i* depends only on `(seed, i)`, so each cell's numbers are identical to running its profile alone.

**Why `z` is secondary.** A null distribution built on degraded graphs is not normal, and for the Erdős–Rényi mode its variance can collapse, producing numerically enormous *z* values that mean only "decisively different".

### S2.1 The three-way verdict and the equivalence margin

Failing to reject a null is not evidence of equivalence, and none of the software's return values says it is. Each mode gets exactly one of `distinguishable`, `equivalent_within_tolerance` or `indeterminate`, against a margin δ prespecified as 5% of the **vehicle readout** of the real graph. The vehicle readout is the right scale because it is in the units of the readout, does not depend on the drug, on the null ensemble or on the number of shuffles, and is the same for every mode of a cell, so an equivalence claim made against it is comparable across the ladder. It is one tenth of the 50 % relative change the selectivity module treats as a circuit response. A cell whose baseline is undefined or zero can form no margin; its non-rejected modes are then `indeterminate`, which is the correct answer rather than a missing one.

**The confirmatory profile in full.** For imidacloprid at 1 µM on `named`, at 1000 permutations against a margin of 0.332 rate-model units, the per-mode probabilities and verdicts are: weight permutation *p* = 0.586, equivalent_within_tolerance; degree-preserving rewiring *p* = 0.472, equivalent_within_tolerance; the weight-matched transmitter null *p* = 0.389, equivalent_within_tolerance; the joint label-permutation null *p* = 0.275, indeterminate; and the Erdős–Rényi control *p* = 9.99e-04, distinguishable. The resulting necessary level is `degree_sequence` with verdict equivalent_within_tolerance.

The **necessary information level** is the weakest rung the test could not distinguish from the real cut, reported with its verdict. Ranks 2 and 3 are not strictly nested — `weight_permute` keeps transmitter identity and loses the weight-topology pairing, the transmitter nulls the reverse — so a richer model can be distinguishable where a weaker one is not. Such cells are **non-monotone** and are flagged: 11 of 84 on the `named` cut (chlorpyrifos_oxon 10 nM, dieldrin 10 nM, dieldrin 100 nM, dieldrin 1 µM, fipronil 10 nM, fipronil 100 nM, ivermectin 1 µM, ivermectin 10 µM, picrotoxin 100 nM, picrotoxin 1 µM, picrotoxin 10 µM) and 0 of 84 on `taste_motor`. Their level must be read as "the cheapest graph model this test cannot tell apart from the real one", never as "everything above it is indistinguishable too".

### S2.2 Effect floor, multiplicity, and confirmatory versus exploratory

**Effect floor.** An absolute floor of 1e-6 Hz against a ~6.6 Hz baseline admits effects at numerical-noise level, so the effective floor is `max(absolute, 1 % × |vehicle readout|)`. Landscapes report class counts under both. On the `named` cut the relative floor moves 18 cells into `no-effect` (acetylcholine 10 nM (0.000761 Hz), acetylcholine 100 nM (0.0118 Hz), ddt 10 nM (0.00309 Hz), ddt 100 nM (0.0309 Hz), gaba 100 nM (-2.74e-05 Hz), gaba 1 µM (-0.000864 Hz), gaba 10 µM (-0.025 Hz), ivermectin 10 nM (-0.0156 Hz), ivermectin 100 nM (-0.0587 Hz), picrotoxin 10 nM (0.0076 Hz), spinosad 10 nM (0.00601 Hz), spinosad 100 nM (0.0549 Hz), sulfoxaflor 10 nM (0.000202 Hz), sulfoxaflor 100 nM (0.00202 Hz), sulfoxaflor 1 µM (0.0196 Hz), thiamethoxam 10 nM (0.000303 Hz), thiamethoxam 100 nM (0.00302 Hz), thiamethoxam 1 µM (0.0289 Hz)), changing the counts from 16 / 55 / 13 to 11 / 42 / 31.

**Multiplicity.** A landscape is a screen. Benjamini-Hochberg is applied across the structural tests of the run (168 on `named`, 168 on `taste_motor`), the class is decided on the adjusted probabilities, and both counts are reported. Because an empirical permutation probability cannot fall below `1/(n+1)`, the result also reports how many tests must sit at that floor before anything can be rejected, so that a corrected count of zero is never mistaken for a finding (`fdr_can_reject` = yes here).

**Confirmatory versus exploratory.** The imidacloprid and fipronil profiles at *n* ≥ 1000 are prespecified planned comparisons and carry no correction; everything else is exploratory and says so in its warnings. Fipronil at 1 µM is the clean illustration: confirmatory *p* = 0.006, but inside the exploratory landscape its adjusted probability is 0.050 and the cell does not survive correction (composition-dominated corrected against topology-dependent raw).

### S2.3 Does the instrument work? Ground-truth recovery and power

Without this, a negative from the ladder is indistinguishable from an under-powered test. A synthetic cut of 250 nodes and about 1500 edges is built with log-normal weights and a transmitter composition close to the real cut's, and a directed cycle of cholinergic nodes passing through the driven seeds is planted in it at a known strength. The cycle is recurrent cholinergic gain, so a patch that collapses `g_ach` removes an amplification that exists only while the cycle is intact: the drug *contrast*, not merely the rate, depends on the wiring. Strength 0 plants nothing and is the negative control. Nothing here touches the connectome or the compound library.

the ladder recovered 2 of 3 planted topology-dependent effects at n = 200 and returned no false positive on the unplanted control. Over the power grid (6 independently generated graphs per cell, planted strengths 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, permutation counts 50, 200, 1000) the detection rate by strength is strength 0.25: 0.00-0.17; strength 0.5: 0.67-0.83; strength 1: 1.00-1.00 and by permutation count n=50: 0.61, n=200: 0.61, n=1000: 0.67; the empirical false-positive rate on the unplanted control is 0 of 18 (0.000). Effect size dominates: raising the permutation count buys far less than raising the planted strength. Full grid: T22, Fig. F16.

**Permutation counts in this document.** 1000 per mode for the `named` profiles (resolution 0.0010), 300 for the `taste_motor` path arm, 1000 for each of the 84 `named` landscape cells and 300 for each of the 84 `taste_motor` cells. The sweep (T11) shows the verdict at α = 0.05 settling from *n* ≈ 50–400, and a mid-range *p* reaching ±0.02 of its final value between *n* = 25 and 400.

### S2.4 Where does the verdict settle with the size of the cut?

The verdict reversed between the two committed cuts, and two cuts are not a trend. `flylab.analysis.scale.dependence_vs_scale` runs the *same* dependence profile (`dependence_profile`, unchanged) across a ladder of nested cuts of 1k, 5k, 10k, 25k, 50k cells grown from the taste-motor seed set by one fixed rule, so a verdict can be compared across size without the construction changing underneath it. The two committed cuts are built by a different recipe, so the clean scale axis is the `scale_*` rungs alone; a sequence that interleaves the committed cuts mixes a size effect with a construction effect, and `verdict_stability(recipe="scale_ladder")` reports each separately. A verdict counts as *settled* only if it is the same on the two largest cuts run with no change above that point — one rung of agreement is n = 1 and is not stability.

Cost is the constraint, not correctness. From the per-shuffle and per-edge constants measured on this machine (T26), a profile at *n* = 1000 on the 50k rung is 21.1 hours on one core and the landscape over the specification family is 7 days per specification; a whole-CNS profile at the weight-5 floor is 59 hours and the dense LIF matrix stops fitting in memory well before it. The permutation count must therefore fall with the cut, and `permutation_budget` refuses to go below the resolution floor `1/α − 1`, so every rung records the *n* it was run at.

The ladder's smallest rung, `scale_1k`, is already the decisive contrast with the two cuts of T19: 1000 nodes, 22 857 edges, mean degree 22.9, in-star no — *fewer* nodes than `named` and a densely recurrent cut nothing like it.

**Result** (T27; the study record is read from `scale_study.json` beside the paper artifacts, or from `data/derived/` when it is promoted there). Over the 7 cuts run (scale_1k, named, taste_motor, scale_5k, scale_10k, scale_25k, scale_50k), the composition-dominated verdict survives on 1 — named — so the inversion does not track the number of cells: `scale_1k` has 1000 nodes against `named`'s 1126 and a mean degree of 22.9 against 1.21, and it is already topology-dependent. Per-rung sequences: scale_1k (1000 nodes, mean degree 22.9): topology-dependent; named (1126 nodes, mean degree 1.2): composition-dominated; taste_motor (1841 nodes, mean degree 10.4): topology-dependent; scale_5k (5000 nodes, mean degree 56.0): topology-dependent; scale_10k (10000 nodes, mean degree 62.2): topology-dependent; scale_25k (25000 nodes, mean degree 54.6): topology-dependent; scale_50k (50000 nodes, mean degree 44.3): topology-dependent. Fipronil is topology-dependent on every rung. In summary: imidacloprid: the verdict is 'topology-dependent' on the largest 5 of 7 cuts tested, first reached at 'taste_motor'. fipronil: the verdict is 'topology-dependent' on the largest 7 of 7 cuts tested, first reached at 'scale_1k'. On the clean `scale_*` ladder the verdicts settle: yes. At scale_5k, scale_10k the necessary information level is the real network itself, i.e. the prediction is distinguishable from *every* rung of the ladder. Imidacloprid's relative effect stays within 0.52-0.93 of the vehicle readout across the ladder, so the verdict changes while the quantity being measured does not.

**Two caveats, both reported rather than absorbed.** The permutation budget falls with the edge count (20-1000 draws, resolution 0.0010-0.0476): at scale_10k, scale_25k, scale_50k the permutation budget falls to n = 20, where the smallest attainable probability is 0.048 against alpha = 0.05: a rejection there is the smallest the test can express, so those rungs confirm a verdict already settled lower down rather than establishing it. And `named` and `taste_motor` are hops-limited neighbourhoods while the `scale_*` rungs are nested ranked-BFS cuts, so a difference *between* the two families confounds scale with construction; differences *within* the `scale_*` rungs do not.

## S3. Runtimes and their calibration

**Rate runtime.** `flylab/circuit/rate.py` holds a dense signed weight matrix `W[j,i] = sign(nt_i) × synapses(i→j)`, with the signs of Table T21. **That matrix is then row-normalised**: `W_signed = W / max(Σ_i |W[j,i]|, 1)`, one denominator per postsynaptic cell. A gain patch afterwards scales the columns by the presynaptic transmitter's gain, so `W_eff = W_signed · diag(g)` and **each cell's recurrent input is a composition-weighted average of its presynaptic gains, normalised by its own total absolute input**. The update is a clipped leaky iteration `r ← clip((1 − α)·r + α·g_nav·(d + W_eff·r), 0, r_max)` with α = 0.3, 80 steps, `r_max` = 300 Hz and *d* the per-node external drive in Hz. It is deterministic, and one evaluation of the 1126-node graph costs about 50 ms with single-threaded BLAS — which is what makes 1000-permutation profiles and an 84-cell landscape affordable at all.

The normalisation is an **asserted modelling choice**, not a measurement, and it is consequential for RQ2: a readout in which each cell averages its presynaptic gains builds part of "composition-dominated" into itself. Three modes are implemented and swept in §3.3 (T25):

| mode | denominator | note |
|---|---|---|
| `row_abs` (shipped) | `max(Σ\|W\|, 1)` | denom[j] = max(sum_i |W[j, i]|, 1): each cell's recurrent input is a composition-weighted average of its presynaptic gains. Shipped default; every published FlyLab number uses it. |
| `none` | 1 | denom[j] = 1: raw signed synapse counts. The recurrent operator is supercritical (spectral radius ~150 on the named cut) and the r_max clip, not the wiring, bounds the rates. |
| `degree` | `max(in-degree, 1)` | denom[j] = max(in-degree[j], 1): degree-corrected. Relative input strengths survive (unlike row_abs) but hubs are discounted by fan-in. |

The composition-versus-full rank correlation at 1 µM is 0.988 under `row_abs`, 0.906 under `none` and 0.666 under `degree` (-0.372 at 10 nM): the composition verdict survives removing the normalisation but not replacing it with a degree-corrected one. Levels are not comparable across modes — the unnormalised operator is supercritical on this cut, so the `r_max` clip rather than the wiring bounds the rates — only the orderings are.

**LIF runtime.** `flylab/circuit/lif.py` is a current-based leaky integrate-and-fire network with the parameters of Shiu *et al.* [7]: τ_m = 20 ms, V_rest = −52 mV, V_th = −45 mV, V_reset = −52 mV, refractory 2.2 ms, exponential synaptic current with τ_s = 5 ms, forward Euler at dt = 0.1 ms. A presynaptic spike on edge *i*→*j* injects `w_scale × synapses × sign(nt_i) × gain(nt_i)` mV into *j*.

Two choices are modelling decisions, not literature values. *The synaptic scale is a one-time calibration scan*: `w_scale` = 0.05 mV per synapse and a drive weight of 0.5 mV per event were chosen once, by scanning, so that vehicle MN9 cells fire in a plausible band under the standard 150 Hz seed drive. No recording was used and no error was minimised against data, so this does not consume the one fitted parameter the project allows — but it is a tuned constant. *A background Poisson drive is required, and it is a choice*: excising a 1-hop neighbourhood removes every input from outside it, so every non-seed cell receives an independent Poisson drive at 0.65 × the seed rate, standing in for the amputated inputs. This is the most consequential free choice in the LIF arm; it sets the operating point and it is why the two engines report sugar-driven MN9 at 0.293 Hz (rate) against 105 Hz (LIF).

## S4. Supporting capabilities

These are implemented, tested and used, but are supporting capabilities rather than contributions of this paper.

**Exposure.** `flylab/pharm/exposure.py` maps a dose and route onto *C(t)* and hence engagement(t) in one compartment. Its constants are class placeholders chosen so that the three routes differ in the direction the literature describes, and are fitted to no *Drosophila* ADME dataset because that dataset does not exist: of the seven quantities such a model needs, `data/literature/fly_pharmacokinetics.yaml` records only metabolite identity as well established [51], [53], [54], [57]. Adult haemolymph volume, adult body mass, cuticular penetration rate and any *Drosophila* elimination half-life are `null`; what is recorded is 25 nL recoverable per adult [56], 174 ± 81 nL in third-instar larvae [55], a water-partitioning measurement [52], an ethanol entry that could not supply a rate [64], and an imidacloprid half-life of 4.5–5 h in *honeybee* [57] — the only verified insect half-life for a library compound. Two structural warnings travel with every profile: haemolymph is the lowest-radioactivity compartment for imidacloprid, so a one-compartment haemolymph model under-estimates CNS exposure [58]; and a substantial metabolic route runs through the gut microbiome, so a single-enzyme clearance term is wrong for this compound [53].

**Genotype.** `flylab/pharm/genotype.py` applies published target-site resistance alleles as multiplicative potency shifts from `data/literature/resistance_alleles.yaml` [12]–[24], enforcing one rule: **a shift applies per compound, never per receptor.** Two published findings make the naive implementation wrong — *Rdl* A301S shifts GABA potency 33.9-fold but fipronil only 1.1-fold on the expressed planthopper receptor [14], and *para* M918T shifts deltamethrin 100-fold while leaving DDT at 1-fold [21]. An allele/compound pair with no sourced number is left unchanged and listed under `unshifted`, never extrapolated. One row is deliberately ugly: *Rdl* A301G in *D. simulans* carries a 20000-fold shift derived from a whole-animal resistance ratio, which drives fipronil engagement to 4.63e-04 and is almost certainly an overstatement of the target-site change [18]. The notebook says so. Panel: T9, F9.

**Mixtures.** `flylab/pharm/mixtures.py` implements Bliss independence [44] and Loewe additivity [47], [48] as explicit null models, following the response-surface framing of Greco *et al.* [45], and selects between them per pair: Loewe where the components share a receptor key, Bliss where their targets are disjoint. The three-case comparison against the honeybee dataset [50] is a qualitative concordance check and not validation. Table T5.

**Expression weighting.** `flylab/pharm/expression.py` relaxes the assumption that every cell expresses the receptor, using class-level statements from single-cell atlases [38]–[43] mapped by hand onto MaleCNS superclasses, and interpolates each node's gain between 1.0 and the full scalar gain. It is a **sensitivity layer and never the primary model**: none of the cited atlases uses MaleCNS superclass labels, so every mapping row is tagged `cross_atlas_inference`; coverage is 0.205 of cells; and receptor expression in identified adult motor neurons is a confirmed gap, so MN9 carries the `unknown` default. Weighting shifts MN9 by -2.622 Hz at 1 µM imidacloprid, which we report as the size of the uncertainty rather than as a correction.

**Model dose-response.** `flylab/assays/ensemble.py` builds the model's own concentration-response curve, fits a four-parameter Hill function by Nelder–Mead in log-concentration space and bootstraps it. For imidacloprid on the `named` cut the circuit midpoint is 34.7 nM with a bootstrap 95 % CI of 35.2 nM to 71.5 nM (r² = 0.9999). The interval does not bracket the point estimate, and that is reported rather than hidden: the bootstrap resamples replicates within each concentration, the lower plateau of the ladder is short, and the midpoint is therefore poorly identified. This is in any case the **model's** circuit midpoint, not a receptor or an animal IC50. The one-at-a-time tornado at 1 µM finds exactly zero span for ec50, hill_n — the engagement is saturated — which the global analysis (§3.5) confirms and quantifies.

## S5. Prospective predictions

The following 7 hypotheses are written before any experiment and labelled `software_prediction`; `live_result` is `null` in every row and stays null until a real table is imported by hand. They are **prospective, not pre-registered**: a mutable repository is not a registry, and the wording will change only if a tagged release is externally archived with a timestamp. Full table: T3.

* **H1** (receptor engagement). At 1 µM, imidacloprid insect nicotinic engagement ≥ 0.9 while vertebrate α4β2 stays ≤ 0.2. Predicted gap 0.854. Checkable against published expressed-receptor work rather than against flies.
* **H2** (PER, `protocols/per.md`). The same dose lowers cholinergic drive in the MN9/DNp01 neighbourhood: predicted change in network mean rate -6.145 Hz. **Specification-dependence caveat.** The *sign* of this prediction is a modelling choice, not a model output: §3.4 shows the same compound at the same engagement takes the network from 6.631 Hz to 0.458 Hz under the shipped biphasic rule and from 6.631 Hz to 10.1 Hz under a monotone rule of the same admissible family, and only 15 of 25 specifications retain the suppression direction. An experimenter should treat H2 as a signed interval over the family rather than as a point prediction, and a live result in either direction falsifies a specification rather than the integration. The control rate this needs could not be sourced; the nearest substitute is 40 % responders at 1 h food deprivation rising to 68 % at 2 h in 463 wild-type flies [60], which also shows that deprivation time is a larger nuisance variable than most effects FlyLab would try to detect.
* **H3** (climbing, `protocols/climbing.md`). Fipronil at 1 µM disinhibits the neighbourhood: predicted change 0.896 Hz, opposite in sign to H2. **The same caveat applies transitively**: H3 is defined relative to H2, so its contrast inherits H2's specification dependence even though the fipronil arm itself is retained by 25 of 25 specifications. Negative geotaxis is the best-served endpoint [59], [63], [65].
* **H4** (negative control). Diazepam engages vertebrate GABA-A and not insect RDL in this library, so it moves no fly readout: predicted effect 0.000. A non-zero live effect would falsify either the library row [16] or the assumption that the compound reaches the CNS unmetabolised.
* **H5** (within-animal control). Bitter input vetoes sugar-driven MN9 on the map-extracted path: predicted change -0.296 Hz relative to sugar alone, matching the published direction [7], [62].
* **H6**. Fipronil relieves the bitter veto on the map path: predicted rise in the veto ratio 0.413. The dependence analysis shows this readout is not distinguishable from its shuffled controls, so a live confirmation would support the pharmacology and **not** the wiring.
* **H7** (selectivity negative control). Picrotoxin perturbs the fly neighbourhood while its receptor selectivity index is ≈ 0: predicted change 0.340 Hz. Any insect-selectivity claim the tool makes must fail for picrotoxin, and picrotoxin's lack of insect-over-vertebrate selectivity is independently attested [30].

Rows where the model's own output disagrees with the prospective direction: none.

**On sample sizes.** Model-internal intervals (6 replicates of library jitter, drive jitter and the RNG seed) contain no biological variance, so the standardised effect sizes they imply are absurd. T3 therefore caps *d* at 1.0 before computing *n*, which yields 23 per group for every row. **This is an illustrative planning minimum, not a power calculation**: it means "what a large effect would need", it assumes independent flies where vial-based protocols violate that, and it must not be used to size a study.

## S6. Evidence typing: the full rules

The permitted transformation is a function of two facts — the **parameter type** the source measured and the **evidence distance** between that source and this compound at this receptor in this species. The distance grades are E0 (on-target); E1 (cross-species); E2 (related receptor); E3 (class extrapolation); E4 (unsupported). The full (parameter type × distance) → model table is generated as T20; its shape is:

| Parameter type | E0 | E1–E2 | E3 | E4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Kd`, `Ki` | `binding_occupancy` | `binding_engagement_proxy` | `functional_engagement_proxy` | `not_modelled` |
| `EC50`, `IC50`, `Kb` | `functional_engagement` | `functional_engagement_proxy` | `functional_engagement_proxy` | `not_modelled` |
| `relative_potency`, `class_order`, `unknown` | `not_modelled` | `not_modelled` | `not_modelled` | `not_modelled` |

A proxy computes the same Hill expression as its non-proxy sibling but reports itself as a transfer and carries a provenance warning naming what was transferred — the species, the preparation or the chemical class. Asking for a binding occupancy from an EC50, or for any numeric engagement from a placeholder row, raises `EvidenceTypeError`; the library validator rejects a placeholder that carries a value and a non-placeholder that does not. The library currently reports none validation problems.

**The soundness invariant.** *No numeric engagement reaching a circuit readout may originate from a row whose (parameter type, evidence distance) pair does not admit one.* `tests/test_pharm_evidence.py` checks it exhaustively rather than at a call site: over every row of the shipped library and every entry point that can produce an engagement, and with a companion check that dropping the inadmissible rows changes no gain vector. Mutating the rule table makes the test fail with a message naming the row and the pair. This, rather than the enum, is what the manuscript claims as a computing contribution.

**Census.** 102 rows over 21 compounds and 15 receptor keys (Table T10, Figure F11). Parameter types: 34 EC50, 10 IC50, 2 Kd, 56 unknown. Evidence distances: 10 E0, 15 E1, 10 E2, 11 E3, 56 E4. Engagement models: 1 `binding_occupancy`, 1 `binding_engagement_proxy`, 9 `functional_engagement`, 35 `functional_engagement_proxy`, 56 `not_modelled`. Rows entitled to a binding-occupancy model: imidacloprid at insect_nAChR_native_dmel (Kd 2e-09 M) [76]. Rows demoted to a binding proxy by distance: imidacloprid at insect_nAChR_beta1 (Kd 8.3e-11 M).

## S8. Model-versus-implementation warnings carried in every notebook

Every assay returns a notebook (schema 0.3) whose `warnings` list ships with the artefact rather than in a console message: hops-limited neighbourhood, predicted (not measured) transmitter signs, teaching-tier potency values, reduced-circuit substitution where it applies, engagement-is-not-occupancy, a provenance warning naming the transfer for every proxy row, and the not-modelled reason for every N/A row. The `provenance` block records the FlyLab version, git commit, library SHA-256, map name and citation, RNG seed and platform. The library hash is what makes the honesty rules auditable: if a number changes, the hash changes, and a notebook produced before the change cannot be confused with one produced after.

## S9. Model-specific caveats in full

These are summarised in §4 and given here at length.

**(i) The transmitter sign table is mostly asserted.** Acetylcholine at +1.0 is the normalisation every other entry is scaled against, and GABA at −1.0 is the convention that inhibition is as strong per synapse as excitation. The remaining five — glutamate -0.4, histamine -0.5, dopamine +0.2, serotonin +0.2, octopamine +0.2 — are asserted magnitudes with no measurement behind them (T21). Glutamate in particular is signed inhibitory at 40 % of the stated strength, which is a hedge against the cells whose glutamatergic output is excitatory rather than a measurement of anything; any such cell is mis-signed in this model. None of these five is a member of the specification family, and the transmitter nulls do not bound them, because they permute *assignments* and leave the table untouched. Separately, the ablation ladder's level B inherits a whole-CNS excitation index that scores glutamate as partly excitatory while level D signs it inhibitory; the two levels of one ladder therefore disagree about a transmitter sign, and reconciling them moves the composition-versus-full rank correlation by at most 0.057.

**(ii) The row normalisation is load-bearing** (§S3, §3.3).

**(iii) Receptor effects are applied across transmitter-defined synapses.** A gain on `g_ach` scales every cholinergic synapse, whether or not that synapse expresses the receptor the compound binds. This is the assumption the expression layer relaxes, and it is reported as a bound rather than an improvement: coverage is 0.205 of cells, every mapping row is a cross-atlas inference, and receptor expression in identified adult motor neurons — MN9's class — is a confirmed gap in the atlas literature, so the paper's principal readout carries the default weight. Weighting shifts MN9 by -2.622 Hz at 1 µM imidacloprid.

**(iv) Hops-limited cuts.** A 1-hop neighbourhood with a weight floor is a fraction of MN9's real input; missing inhibition can flip the sign of any readout, and the disinhibition readout is bounded by the GABA share that survived the cut. Growing the cut is deliberately constrained: past about 1 MB of JSON the weight floor is raised rather than hops added, because a 2-hop cut of a motor neuron in a 25-million-edge CNS is most of the animal. §3.2 is the quantitative demonstration of how much the choice of cut matters — the dependence verdict itself reverses between the two committed cuts.

**(v) The two cuts differ in what they can express.** The `named` cut carries 612 cholinergic, 295 GABAergic and 56 glutamatergic cells, and contains 0 octopaminergic cells and 0 on `taste_motor`, so a compound whose only insect mechanism acts through `g_oct` cannot move either readout for substrate reasons rather than pharmacological ones; 14% of `named` cells carry no consensus transmitter prediction and are presynaptically inert. Full structural census: T19.

**(vi) The rate-versus-LIF discrepancy** (§3.6) is agreement of direction only, and follows from the LIF background drive of §S3.

## S10. Domain readouts, selectivity and literature concordance

These are the domain-side results the method is demonstrated on. They are supporting material: none of them is a contribution of this paper, and §3 evaluates the instruments rather than these numbers.

**The evidence census in full.** Of 102 rows over 21 compounds and 15 receptor keys, 34 carry an EC50, 10 an IC50 and 2 a binding constant. By evidence distance the split is 10 / 15 / 10 / 11 / 56 rows at E0–E4. At the 1 µM working concentration the two headline compounds engage their insect targets at 0.991 and 0.985 against 0.091 and 0.476 at the vertebrate counterparts (500-fold and 37-fold; 2.70 log₁₀ units of window for the first). Placeholder rows contribute nothing: the negative-control row reports "not modelled (N/A)".

**Sensory-veto readout.** Driving the 34 sweet GRN body IDs at 150 Hz and reading MN9 reproduces the published direction of a bitter veto [7], [62] on both runtimes (0.000 rate, 0.000 LIF in vehicle; 0.413 and 0.159 under fipronil). The two runtimes agree on **direction only** — sugar-driven MN9 is 0.293 Hz on the rate engine against 105 Hz on the LIF engine, a difference following from the LIF background drive (§S3) — and we do not present that agreement as cross-model validation.

**A properly powered negative.** At 300 permutations the veto ratio on the extracted sensory path is not distinguishable from any shuffled control (max |z| = 0.220), equivalent within tolerance for none and indeterminate for erdos_renyi, rewire_degree_preserving, sign_permute, sign_permute_weight_matched, weight_permute. Because §3.1 characterises the instrument's power, this is reportable as a negative rather than as an absence of evidence. The comparator compound has no veto ratio at this dose at all — the readout is *undefined*, not zero, and is reported as undefined.

**Selectivity landscape.** The split is by mechanism rather than potency: chlorpyrifos_oxon, ddt, deltamethrin, permethrin are amplified by the circuit and acetamiprid, acetylcholine, clothianidin, imidacloprid, nicotine, nitenpyram buffered, subject to the threshold caveat of §3.4. Compounds never reaching the circuit threshold on either cut (chlordimeform, dieldrin, fipronil, gaba, ivermectin, picrotoxin, spinosad, sulfoxaflor, thiamethoxam) are reported **unscored** rather than buffered, and one of them is unscored for a substrate reason rather than a pharmacological one: chlordimeform's only insect mechanism acts through octopamine, and neither cut contains an octopaminergic cell.

**Literature concordance** is reported per entry with its *n* (T4) and never as a bare mean: of 8 evaluable entry–assay combinations, 4 order exactly two compounds, where Spearman's ρ can only be ±1 whatever the model does, and over the 4 entries with more than two the mean ρ is 0.756. As a concordance count the result is 8 concordant against 0 discordant orderings (two-sided binomial *p* = 0.008); 16 combinations are skipped for want of coverage and 2 inversions are recorded rather than repaired (nitenpyram_rank_inverted, clothianidin_vs_imidacloprid_inverted). Of the 6 distinct published orderings the library can cover, 1 comes from a publication the library already cites for a compound in that ordering and is therefore *not* out-of-sample, while 5 are source-disjoint. None is an independent test of the *circuit* model. The mixture arm is a **limited qualitative concordance check** (3 of 3 verdicts reproduced, reference organism Apis mellifera, 2 partners same-class substitutes, §S4).
