## Scientific boundaries retained

- Dependence labels remain conditional on extract, readout, runtime, null family and statistical effort; recurrence is not isolated causally.
- A point-gap check after non-rejection is not statistical equivalence.
- The synthetic grid is limited positive-control evidence, not general calibration or endpoint-specific power.
- Composition/full agreement is reported with its matched reference and normalisation dependence.
- Nicotinic suppression remains gain-rule dependent, and threshold classifications remain unstable.
- Expression mapping coverage remains `{{expr_coverage_overall}}`, with MN9 unresolved.
- There is no independent circuit-level or live-animal validation, no animal dose prediction and no cross-species safety claim.
- The scale table remains a replayed saved study, and the latest full verification attempt was interrupted during dependence analysis.
